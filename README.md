# Dholakpurr

A block-built travel journal. Visitors walk through a stylised map of northern India (Himalayas, Kashmir, Himachal,
Delhi, Agra, Jaipur, Udaipur), press **E** for a parchment map, travel to any place, and open the **Wayfare Safe**
beside it to read your photos, stories and lessons.

Pure static files (HTML, CSS, JS). No build step, no dependencies. Needs a browser with WebGL2.

## Add your photos and stories

Everything you write lives in **`js/content.js`**.

1. Copy your photos into `assets/photos/`.
2. Open `js/content.js`. Each place has an id (listed at the top of the file). Add pages like this:

```js
udaipur: {
  entries: [
    { photo: 'assets/photos/udaipur-1.jpg', title: 'Golden hour', date: 'March 2025',
      caption: 'Lake Pichola', story: 'What happened...', learning: 'What it taught me...' }
  ]
}
```

Visitors turn pages by scrolling, with the arrow keys, or with the buttons. Places you leave out still have a
Wayfare Safe with a friendly "add your story here" page. Change the world title and tagline at the top of the file.

## What's in the world

- **Map (E):** parchment overview with a pin for every place and a "Wayfare Log" list. Click to travel.
- **Wayfare Safes:** one beside every arrival point, with a "Click here to open" prompt.
- **Day and sunset only** in the normal world. Find the glowing purple **portal** in the Taj Mahal garden and stand
  inside it to visit the **night side** (fireflies, sky lanterns, aurora, glowing lamps). Walk back in to return.
- **Animals:** cows, buffalo, goats, sheep, dogs, horses, camels, elephants, yaks and peacocks, plus butterflies by day.
- **Villages** in every region, with wells, lamp posts and fields.
- **Views (P):** first person, third person, front view. **Five looks** to choose from; your own is the default.
- **Key hint pop-ups** in an achievement-toast style (turn off in Settings, or press H for the full guide).
- **Shaders:** sun shadows, ambient occlusion, bloom, god rays, animated water, clouds, aurora, ACES tone mapping.
  Presets: Balanced, Cinematic, Vivid, Performance.

## Controls

WASD move, Space jump (tap twice to fly), Ctrl sprint, Shift fly down, E map, P view, H help, left/right click
break/place (or open a safe), 1-0 or wheel pick slot, Z/X change the block in a slot, T drift the sun, V/B shadows/bloom,
R back to start, Esc pause.

## Deploy on GitHub Pages

1. Create a repository and upload everything from this folder to the root.
2. Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > `main` and `/ (root)` > Save.
3. Your site goes live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

Netlify, Vercel and Cloudflare Pages also work with no build command.

## Files

- `index.html`, `style.css`: screens and styling
- `js/content.js`: **your photos and stories** (the file you edit)
- `js/world.js`: terrain, landmarks, villages, vault and portal placement
- `js/entities.js`: animals, avatar, fireflies, lanterns, butterflies
- `js/skins.js`: playable looks
- `js/ui.js`: loading, title, map, book, skin picker, hints
- `js/main.js`: renderer, shaders, player

Everything is generated by code: no third-party maps, textures or models. Not affiliated with Mojang, Microsoft or the
authors of any shader pack.
